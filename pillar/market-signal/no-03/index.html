Phoenix renewals are negative while the nation stays positive

Renewal pricing in Phoenix fell below last year's level while national renewal growth held above zero (+1.7%). For a renewal conversation, the comparison that matters may now be today's advertised rent, not last year's lease.

Section two

The Local Reality

In Phoenix, advertised asking rents are down about two percent over the year and renewal pricing is now below zero too. Yardi notes that renewal growth is fading in some markets as existing rents catch up to advertised rents. For a resident, that comparison is one search away: today's advertised price for their own floor plan.

Seattle: no verified renewal figures were reviewed for this edition, so we are not drawing a Seattle conclusion here.

Section three

The Operator Decision

Before the next batch of renewal offers goes out, three questions worth answering at each community:

aHow does each renewal offer compare with the advertised rent for the same floor plan today, specials included?
bWhat does a move out actually cost here once vacant days, make ready and any concession are counted?
cDo residents get enough notice to decide before they start shopping elsewhere?
