How stories are chosen

Each story is scored on what moves an apartment P&L: rents, occupancy and concessions, supply, debt and rates, insurance and taxes, jobs and policy. Arizona and Seattle stories get extra weight. Duplicates, luxury home listings and other property types are left out.

Nothing tagged Seattle cleared the desk this cycle. Local stories are kept for up to 45 days, so this fills as the publishers file.
