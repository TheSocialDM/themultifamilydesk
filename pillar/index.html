About Melanie

Melanie has spent more than three decades in multifamily. Since 2000 she has served as President of Pillar Communities, leading operations across Phoenix and Seattle: portfolio performance, resident experience, team development and operational strategy, with close involvement in acquisition and disposition decisions.

Before Pillar she spent eleven years at Archstone as a district manager, responsible for property operations, leasing, resident satisfaction and budgets across her portfolio.
