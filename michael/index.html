    Plan for answers, not just rankings

    More renter questions get answered inside Google and AI assistants. Budget for being the community those answers name, not only for page one.

    Treat your Google profile like a leasing office

    Hours, specials, photos, floor plans and replies are often the first tour. Keep it current every week, not every quarter.

    Make reviews a weekly habit

    Recent, specific reviews and thoughtful replies are trust signals people and AI both read. Ask at move in, after maintenance, and at renewal.

    Measure leases, not likes

    Owners are watching every dollar. Report on tours and leases tied to each channel, and cut what cannot show its part.

    Run one plan across every channel

    Social, Google, reputation and AI search work best when they say the same thing. Separate vendors with separate plans send mixed signals.
